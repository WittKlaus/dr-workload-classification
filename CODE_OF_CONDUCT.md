# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the
people who rely on this tool in stressful disaster-recovery situations. Harassment,
discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or discussions.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can be sent to the maintainer listed in the README. Reports are
handled confidentially.
